How the plan flows

Four required workouts rotate freely; phases advance as you complete them.

Flexible rotation

Instead of fixed weekdays, train in this order whenever it suits your week:

1. Upper A2. Lower A3. Upper B4. Lower B

Completing all four advances your training phase automatically. Skipping a day simply continues from the same point later.

Four-phase cycle

Phase 1 AdaptationPhase 2 ProgressionPhase 3 ProductivePhase 4 Recovery

Three progressive phases are followed by one easier phase, then the cycle repeats — always tied to completed workouts, never the calendar.

Duration modes

  • Express (40–50 min): essential exercises only
  • Standard (60–75 min): essential + recommended
  • Complete (75–90 min): full session including optional work

Exercise swaps

Every exercise has approved machine-based alternatives — pick whichever equipment is free or feels best that day. Free-weight squats and deadlifts are intentionally excluded in favor of machine variations.

Execution guide

Simple rules that keep the plan safe and effective.

RIR made simple

  • 4 RIR: ~4 clean reps remained
  • 2 RIR: ~2 remained
  • 1 RIR: ~1 remained
  • 0 RIR: failure — avoided by default

Progression

Keep the same load until every set reaches the top of its range at the assigned RIR, then increase by the smallest available increment.

Low readiness days

Use the Easy recommendation, reduce load ~10–15%, or switch to Express mode. A short walk is a fine substitute if energy is very low.

When to seek care

Sharp, radiating, or worsening joint pain, dizziness, chest discomfort, or symptoms that persist should be assessed by a healthcare professional.

Safety note

This is general fitness guidance, not medical treatment. Stop training and seek qualified medical assessment for chest pain, dizziness, unusual shortness of breath, sharp pain, or symptoms that persist or worsen.
